## Text 1：经济 · The Uneven Revival of the World Economy

> 现实依据：改编自 2025–26 年 IMF《世界经济展望》与 OECD 全球债务数据的公开观点——通胀回落与财政赤字并存，AI 热潮推高股市而制造业利润承压；关税与非关税配额重振贸易壁垒；发达国家房贷利率反复。

**用词统计**：正文使用目标词 **31** 个（A 15｜B 1｜C2 14｜C1 1），D 类词 0 个。

The prospect (前景) of a durable global revival (复苏) has brightened, yet the recovery remains sharply uneven across regions and households. Headline inflation has receded in most advanced economies; the fiscal deficit (财政赤字) of many governments, however, keeps widening, and the accounting (会计/核算) devices used to disguise it only compound (使加剧) the problem. Optimists point to buoyant equity (股权) markets and record corporate revenue (收入); critics counter that prosperity (繁荣) is being redistributed rather than broadly shared, and that a thin commercial margin (利润率/边缘) separates record profits from a sudden reversal.

The evidence lends weight to both readings. A handful of technology giants now exercise near-monopoly (垄断) power over key infrastructure (基础设施), from cloud servers to battery lines, leaving smaller rivals (竞争对手) to compete on unfavorable terms. Renewed trade quotas (配额) and tariffs have raised the cost of bulk (大宗) commodities (商品), while domestic (国内的) manufacturers strain to compensate (补偿) through thinner margins — or through creative turnover (周转) counting that flatters the books. Even the supermarket aisle (货架走道) tells the story: shelf prices rose faster than wages, shrinking the real budgets of ordinary consumers.

Deficits (赤字) deserve overdue scrutiny. Mortgage (抵押贷款) rates, initially eased by central banks, have tightened again, so that financing a home now consumes a startling share of income; the scarcity (稀缺) of affordable housing compounds (加剧) household insecurity. Governments meanwhile borrow freely, hoping to curb (抑制) inflation without dampening the surge (激增) in business expectations. The risk, as so often in history, is that irrational (非理性的) exuberance escapes the discipline of facts and a speculative boom ends in a general bust.

Three remedies would help. First, an independent commission (委员会) with real investigative power to audit public accounts and eliminate (消除) creative accounting would restore honesty to fiscal debate, measuring the deficit against the revenue that taxpayers can realistically provide. Second, antitrust policy should facilitate (促进) market entry and diminish (减少) monopolistic bottlenecks. Third, regulators in different countries should cooperate with their counterparts (对应方/同行) to prevent currency wars and to monitor leveraged (杠杆式的) bets by large financial institutions.

History is not reassuring on this score. In earlier booms, leveraged borrowing financed municipal excess (过量/过剩), and the resulting bust imposed a decade of hardship on everyone except the fortunate few who had sold early. The lesson is not that markets are evil but that discipline is everything: calm supervision, transparent accounts, and honest pricing remain the enduring foundation for prosperity.

**【长难句拆解】**

- **The prospect of a durable global revival has brightened, yet the recovery remains sharply uneven across regions and households.**  
  主干 = prospect has brightened, yet recovery remains uneven（yet 表转折的两个并列分句）；① of a durable global revival 后置定语修饰 prospect；② across regions and households 状语限定恢复不均的范围；译文可先译长主语的中心词 prospect 再挂修饰。
- **A handful of technology giants now exercise near-monopoly power over key infrastructure, from cloud servers to battery lines, leaving smaller rivals to compete on unfavorable terms.**  
  主干 = giants exercise power over…；① A handful of 挺「一小撮」，表量的引出语；② from…to… 说明 infrastructure 的涵盖面；③ leaving…to compete 现在分词作结果状语——译文处理为「令（使）小对手在不利条件下竞争」。
- **The risk, as so often in history, is that irrational exuberance escapes the discipline of facts and a speculative boom ends in a general bust.**  
  主干 = The risk is that…（表语从句）；① as so often in history 插入语，意为「正如历史上屡见不鲜」；② 从句内两个并列谓语 escapes…and ends…；③ 译文把插入语提前——「历史上屡见不鲜的风险在于……」。

## Text 2：AI 与工作 · Clerical Work in the Age of the Algorithm

> 现实依据：改编自 2024–26 年公开报道与研究的共识——欧盟《人工智能法案》自 2024 年 8 月起分阶段生效；科技行业裁员潮（Layoffs.fyi 统计）；多项调查显示白领已普遍使用生成式 AI；劳动经济学对自动化冲击「常规任务」的经典判断。

**用词统计**：正文使用目标词 **28** 个（A 8｜B 2｜C2 14｜C1 4），D 类词 0 个。

The spread of generative artificial intelligence in barely two years has been stunning (惊人的). Once a curious gadget (小器物) confined to hobbyists, it has become a fixture (常设之物) of the modern office, and the momentum (势头) of adoption shows no sign of slowing. Employees now retrieve (调取) records, draft replies, and summarize meetings with software that works simultaneously (同时地) and tirelessly; the feat (壮举) is genuine, and no amount of scepticism can send the machine back into the box. The honest question is not whether the tools will matter, but who will gain from them first.

The consequences for clerical labour are inevitable (不可避免的). Routine work — data entry, scheduling, first-line correspondence — is being automated at a radical (激进的/根本的) pace, and the dismissals (解雇) that follow have already reshaped recruitment patterns. Yet the picture is more complex than a simple transfer of tasks to machines. Algorithms excel (擅长) at pattern-matching but struggle with accountability: they can retrieve an archive (档案) perfectly while missing the intent behind the request. Work that demands judgement, negotiation, or the unspoken exchange of trust remains stubbornly human, and for that reason the indispensable (不可或缺的) worker of the coming decade is the one who can forge (锻造) context — who knows why a document matters, not merely where it is stored.

Some commentators urge a wholly different reading. Routine, they argue, is precisely what the machine compels (迫使) us to surrender, and the initiative (主动性/举措) to reinvent work belongs to whoever can strive (奋斗) rather than loaf (偷懒). Managers who indulge (沉迷) their own hype will be surprised; commuters (通勤者) who reclaim their travel hours and consultants who initiate (启动) new products from a single prompt all prove the same point: the tools multiply output only when humans supply the intention. Whether the gains outweigh (超过) the losses depends less on the software than on the scenario (情形) society chooses to build around it.

Policy can push the balance in a constructive direction. Regulating algorithmic hiring, where bias is easily encoded at scale, is feasible (可行的) and overdue; protecting creative work from piracy (盗版) while allowing fair adaptation deserves legal imagination; and where dismissal is unavoidable, retraining deserves public money rather than sympathetic headlines. Education, moreover, is the single most powerful lever (杠杆) for softening the blow; ventures that thrive (兴旺) on constant innovation reward exactly the skills that machines cannot yet claim. Nobody should pretend the transition is painless, and the stunning (惊人的) speed of change is itself a warning against complacency. But neither should we accept the predictions of mass unemployment as mechanically as an accountant multiplies columns. The worthy question is not whether the machine will change work, but how soon we will radically (彻底地) revise our own habits to change it well.

**【长难句拆解】**

- **Once a curious gadget confined to hobbyists, it has become a fixture of the modern office, and the momentum of adoption shows no sign of slowing.**  
  主干 = it has become a fixture, and momentum shows no sign（两个并列分句，and 连接）；① Once…hobbyists 过去分词短语作状语，交代时间背景，it 指 AI 工具；② of the modern office / of adoption 均为介词短语后置定语；③ 译文先译背景状语——「起初只是限于爱好者的新奇小物，如今已成为现代办公室的常设之物」。
- **Work that demands judgement, negotiation, or the unspoken exchange of trust remains stubbornly human, and for that reason the indispensable worker of the coming decade is the one who can forge context.**  
  主干 = Work remains human, and the worker is the one（and 并列两个分句）；① that…exchange of trust 定语从句修饰 work；② for that reason 因此，承接前句；③ who can forge context 定语从句修饰 the one；④ 译文把 the one 译作「那种……的人」以显代词所指。
- **The worthy question is not whether the machine will change work, but how soon we will radically revise our own habits to change it well.**  
  主干 = The question is not whether…, but how soon…（not…but…对比结构）；① 两个 whether/how soon 引导表语从句；② to change it well 不定式表目的；③ 译文保留「不是……而是……」的对比语势。

## Text 3：健康老龄化 · The Ageing Society: A Manageable Challenge

> 现实依据：改编自联合国《世界人口展望》、世卫组织身体活动指南与日本「超高龄社会」经验的公开观点——生命周期延长、慢病负担上升；墨西哥等国的含糖饮料税已见成效；预防性医疗投入不足的普遍批评。

**用词统计**：正文使用目标词 **21** 个（A 4｜B 0｜C2 14｜C1 3），D 类词 0 个。

Humanity is ageing almost everywhere. Falling birth rates and rising life expectancy (预期寿命) have produced demographies that no previous century ever saw; by the middle of this century, older people will outnumber children in several advanced societies. Japan, the vanguard of this transition, already devotes a quarter of its annual output to health and pensions, and its experience has become a laboratory for the rest of the world.

The true hazards of ageing are not the years themselves but the chronic conditions that accompany them. Heart failure, diabetes, and the steady deterioration (恶化) of organs take their toll, though modest measures can alleviate (缓解) the burden; even the oldest citizen, no longer agile, must contend with the soreness (酸痛) of ageing joints—as the surrounding cartilage deteriorates (恶化)—and the fatigue (疲惫) that no remedy fully removes. Yet the evidence shows that much of this decay is postponable. Modest exercise, a diet low in added sugars, vaccines, screening, and honest labelling postpone frailty and reduce mortality (死亡率); the epidemic (流行病) of sedentary living — office chairs, screens, and delivered meals — is, in a real sense, an avoidable affliction, if not an easily escaped one.

Prevention, disappointingly, remains a fringe concern. Physicians are often reluctant (不情愿的) to prescribe (开具处方) lifestyle change when a pill is simpler, and patients who are susceptible (易受影响) to viral claims may neglect (忽视) the simple rhythms of sleep and food. Insurance coverage (覆盖范围) rarely rewards the vigorous (精力充沛的) patient who bicycles to work, while drug advertising induces (诱导) demand that nobody asked for; a prescription (处方) for a statin arrives more easily than an hour of exercise suggested by the same doctor, and the adverse (不利的) effects of polypharmacy increasingly compound the very pain they were meant to soothe. Diet deficiencies (缺乏) in poorer households — iron, vitamin D, even clean water — shorten life far more reliably than any single disease, and a sugary beverage (饮料) tax, however controversial, has reduced consumption wherever it has been tried. Spouses (配偶) are in truth the primary care system; marriages that endure for decades quietly postpone institutionalisation, and the bereaved often recall that the deceased (已故者) spent their final months at home in a familiar chair, attended by the family's inborn (天生的) tenderness rather than by machines.

None of this is grounds for despair. Small, cheap interventions — walking, screening, vaccination, honest food labelling — repeated at population scale outrun almost every costly cure, and a society that builds parks instead of parking lots may discover that fewer of its citizens are plagued (困扰) by chronic disasters because their bodies were never starved of movement. ## Text 7：科研素养 · The Credibility Gap in Science

> 现实依据：改编自 2024–25 年间「Data Colada」研究者披露的诚信实验数据造假与大规模撤稿事件、心理学「复制危机」文献及学界对预注册（pre-registration）改革的讨论——发现效应量与复现率的双重危机推动方法学改革。

**用词统计**：正文使用目标词 **34** 个（A 11｜B 1｜C2 18｜C1 4），D 类词 0 个。

In a single decade, the phrase "replication crisis" has migrated from specialists' jargon (行话) into ordinary conversation. The discovery that many celebrated findings could not be reproduced has placed entire disciplines under a harsh, overdue scrutiny, and the controversy (争议) over thresholds of statistical significance is as old as statistics itself. What was once accepted on authority is now judged by an uncomfortable rule: expertise (专业知识) is no excuse for sleight of hand. A celebrated researcher whose honesty experiments were found to rest on fabricated data saw dozens of papers retracted; the discrepancy (不一致) between the numbers he published and the numbers he could produce grew, at length, too large to ignore.

The damage is real but uneven. Outright fraud is rare; error is common, and the most prevalent (普遍的) sin is sloppiness dressed as rigour. Researchers too seldom report the peculiar (奇特的) results, the outlier datum (数据点) that could overturn a favourite model; many still "p-hack" their way to a p-value below the conventional criterion (标准) of significance and then attribute (归因于) their findings to a brilliant hypothesis rather than to chance. Journalists, for their part, exaggerate (夸大) effect sizes and depict (描绘) mere correlations (相关性) as causes; readers who demand no less of such claims would do well to ask who paid for the study, whether it compared like with like, and whether the finding reappears elsewhere — questions the answers to which are apparently (显然) still unknown to many who report science as sport. A single striking example illustrates (说明) the problem plainly, and regarding (关于) such cases, the honest conclusion is often that the original result, however bizarre (离奇的), was never truly authentic (真实的) science.

Reform can begin with the mundane. Funding agencies that demand pre-registration and adequate (足够的) statistical power force honesty into the open; journals that publish null results remove the incentive to hide them; a sufficient (充分的) supply of independent replications converts guesswork into knowledge. Some sceptics assert (断言) that such changes are utopian; many practitioners, by contrast, deem (认为) them plainly overdue, and they can point to disciplines that adopted pre-registration and saw their false-positive rate fall markedly — arguably (可以说) the cleanest scientific victory of the past two decades. None of this bolsters (支撑) the fashionable claim that truth is merely a matter of opinion; on the contrary, it affirms that a finding should be judged by its ability to recur. Researchers who speculate (猜测) about the mechanisms behind an effect must first ascertain (查明) that the effect really exists; a poll (民调) of respondents (受访者) measures opinion, not reality, and a "study" built on an irrelevant (不相关的) sample cannot be rescued by elaborate (精心的) statistics, however elaborately the methods are written. The craft of deriving (derive 推导) sound conclusions from sound data is slow, humble work — the very opposite of the illusion (幻象) that prestige can shortcut it.

The deeper point is that credibility is inherent (内在的) to knowledge rather than appended to it. A field that cannot assure the truth of its methods will forfeit public trust whatever the brilliance of its intuition; the alternative — submission to fashion, authority, or sponsorship — has always signified (表明) the passing of learning. Science is inherently (内在地) revisionary; it can afford no polls, no professional shrug, no half-truths about what it does not yet know. It can afford only the truth, verified independently, recorded transparently, and — perhaps most radical of all — corrected promptly; and the public that demands no less of it is not its enemy but its lifelong creditor.

## Text 8：法治品格 · The Wire of Obligation

> 现实依据：改编自 2025 年以来美国联邦法院针对多项行政命令签发临时禁令所引发的宪法争论、国际法学界关于条约约束力的讨论及公民教育对法治传承的长期呼吁——焦点在行政权与司法权之间的制衡与自律。

Democracy, it is often said, rests not on ballots alone but on the rule of law. In practice that means an arrangement of independent courts, written codes, and disciplined officials who honour outcomes they dislike. When a government respects an unfavourable judgment, it concedes (承认) a legal point; when it refuses to do so, the whole architecture of rights begins to sway, and recent confrontations between executives and courts have given the world a live laboratory of both responses.

Three virtues sustain a lawful order. The first is adherence to procedure: obligations (义务) must be created and enforced through due process, by agencies that can be called to account, and a court or bureau (局/署) that issues a rule should do so transparently, open to public comment and judicial challenge. The second is constraint (约束): the natural inclination to accumulate power finds its antidote in constitutional checks — courts that may intervene (干预) against abuse of executive discretion, legislatures that refuse to delegate blank cheques, and a press that will not look away. The third is the discipline (自律/纪律) of office itself: officials who adhere (遵守) to their oaths, agencies that respect the boundary between rule-making and favour, and ministries that concede (承认) error when error has been shown. On each of these hinges, the conduct of any government is the object of legitimate (合法的/合理的) public scrutiny.

Judges are not infallible, and their mistakes can hinder (阻碍) legitimate policy; a judicial overreach that strikes down a popular statute may provoke a public backlash and a constitutional dialogue about the proper scope of review. But the remedy for one overreach is better-reasoned opinions and a legal culture that treats precedent (先例) as a living tradition to be distinguished rather than discarded. Where judges and politicians respect this bargain, politics keeps its equilibrium; where each side tries to override the other by force or procedural trick, the weakening of institutions becomes its own precedent. Governments should therefore oppose (反对) the temptation to pack the courts or spin administrative rules to advantage; a constitution survives its crises precisely because its actors constrain (约束) themselves in good times.

The international dimension is no less exacting. Bilateral (双边的) treaties, regional accords (协议), and multilateral bodies all assume that signed words are honoured; a government that repudiates an agreement it dislikes borrows the currency of its own obligations, and such a ministry, in the long run, pays for its impatience. At home, a civic (公民的) education that teaches the young why law matters — not merely what it forbids — remains the surest long-term investment in both autonomy (自主权) and order. Companies and institutional entities (实体) should likewise be judged by the rigorous (严谨严格的) standard of their internal accountability, for the market punishes the firm whose relaxed culture permits itself too much.

The deep trait (特质) at work is the capacity for self-restraint, that cardinal virtue (美德) of institutions which no statute can import. When power concedes to evidence, when officials admit error, when judges concede a point in return for their own authority, politics behaves less like battle and more like craft — a craft that nonetheless demands constant endeavor (努力) from every generation, and whose practitioners, disciplined in method and moderate in temper, know that the law's authority rests, ultimately, on the day-to-day care given to it.
